# Domain notes (FreshCart Rewards points)
- "Points I spent", "voucher", "redemption", "converted to AeroMiles" all refer to redemptions.
- When the customer identifies a disputed redemption, match it to the allowed choices by date, points, store or description.
- "My husband/wife/partner might have used it" is not a clear yes or no to "still disputing" unless they say whether they still dispute it.
